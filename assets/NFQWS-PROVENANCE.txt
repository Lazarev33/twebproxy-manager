adjacent assets directory. In that state, the first explicit nfqws activation
downloads the official pinned release archive over HTTPS, extracts only the
fixed linux-x86_64 member into a private temporary directory, limits and checks
its exact size, verifies the pinned binary SHA-256 before any execution, and
then verifies the exact version/commit line and approved --dry-run arguments.
